Please don't send your records

Flight records can contain sensitive information and other people's names. Don't attach forms, photos or screenshots of your records. Instead describe the problem: which screen, what you expected, what you saw, and counts or totals if they don't match.

Your data

Your logbook stays on your iPhone. There is no account and nothing to recover from a server: keep your iPhone backed up. Deleting the app deletes its data.

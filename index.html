Immelmann Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Immelmann is a pilot logbook that runs entirely on your iPhone.

What the app collects

Nothing. The developer does not collect, receive, store or sell any data from the app. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no network access.

What stays on your device

  • Logbook entries you type or import (dates, aircraft, routes, flight times).
  • Photos and PDFs you choose to import are read on your iPhone to extract flight times. The app keeps the extracted entries, not the original files. Names of other crew members on imported forms are not stored.
  • Your display preferences (theme, light/dark).

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone. It is included in your own device backups (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on. The developer has no access to those backups.

Sharing

The app shares data only when you tap Copy or Share, and only to the destination you pick. The developer never receives it.

Photos and files

The app opens only the photos or files you select in the system picker. It cannot browse your photo library or files on its own.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
